Sources & what is confirmed

The trophy list is official

The 55 trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list, frozen on 2026-11-18. Every name, description, grade and hidden flag was taken from the official list on Exophase, with each grade and PSN's progress target checked against PSNProfiles. The two agree on every name, description and grade: 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden.

The guides are our own advice

Every guide in this app is our own advice built from the official description of its trophy, not yet checked in the game. We had not played the game when this guide was written. Where the official description does not pin a place down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location. Where a trophy's trigger is unclear from the description alone, the guide says that too, and gives you the most likely way to test it.

What else is confirmed

The game is Grand Theft Auto VI, set in Leonida with Vice City at its heart, starring Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — all officially announced by Rockstar Games. Everything else in the guides (routes, loadouts, timings) is our advice, not Rockstar's word.

No Rockstar artwork

This guide uses no Rockstar logo and no official artwork. The look is our own.
